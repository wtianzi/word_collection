Quantum entanglement remains one of the most counterintuitive predictions of
modern physics. When two particles become entangled, their properties are
inextricably correlated, regardless of the distance separating them. Einstein
famously derided this as "spooky action at a distance," yet subsequent
experiments have unequivocally confirmed the phenomenon. Contemporary
researchers are now harnessing entanglement to develop unprecedented
computational architectures and impregnable cryptographic protocols.
