# Terror Infinity

by zhttty

## Synopsis

Ever since Zheng Zha lost the childhood sweetheart he had loved more than anyone, the dull repetitions of modern life had become unbearable to him. Then, one day, a message appeared on his computer screen:

“Do you want to understand the meaning of life? Do you want to truly… live?”

The moment he clicked YES, everything changed.

He entered an endless cycle of horror movies—the realm known as Lord God's Dimension. There, the only way to survive each new nightmare was to keep growing stronger, to keep evolving.

How would Zheng Zha stay alive? Would he kill everyone who stood against him and walk the lonely path of a conqueror? Or would he fight beside his companions to the bitter end, facing every ordeal with loyalty and honor?

In truth, everything they did was simply to survive—until they discovered the secret of Lord God's Dimension.

Who was the real threat, hidden behind it all?

The answer lies in *Terror Infinity*.
