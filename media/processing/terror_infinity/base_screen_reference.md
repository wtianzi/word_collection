# Terror Infinity — Canon and Translation Reference

This is the authoritative living reference for the English translation of `wuxiankongbu.txt`. The source is GB18030/GBK-encoded and must remain untouched. Translation reads from the ordered UTF-8 batches in `decoded/`; `decoded/manifest.json` is the batching authority. Entries marked **locked** must be used consistently; entries marked **provisional** require later-source confirmation.

## Project conventions

- English title: *Terror Infinity* (**locked**)
- Author credit: zhttty
- Chinese personal names use pinyin without tone marks, family name first.
- Narrative tense: past.
- Dialogue: curly double quotation marks.
- Movie titles and characters use official English release names.
- `主神` → **Lord God**; `主神空间` → **Lord God's Dimension** (**locked**, established genre terminology)
- `恐怖片` → **horror movie** when discussing a film; **scenario** when referring to the lived mission as a game/system unit.
- System-facing terms use concise game language, without adding UI formatting absent from the source.

## Voice and style

- Zheng Zha's early narration should feel emotionally numb rather than melodramatically florid; action prose becomes faster and more visceral as his will to live returns.
- Zhang Jie speaks bluntly, cynically, and with the authority of traumatic experience.
- Chu Xuan's dialogue should be exact, emotionally spare, and logically structured.
- Preserve the novel's escalating survival-horror pace, but split overloaded Chinese sentences when necessary for clean English action.
- Do not introduce fandom explanations into the prose. Ambiguous system lore must remain ambiguous until revealed.
- Explicit sexual material involving Luo Li while she is under eighteen is not reproduced. Preserve plot-relevant facts and emotional consequences non-graphically and mark the omitted explicit passage; never silently change her stated age.

## Continuity ledger

- Entry prompt: “Do you want to understand the meaning of life? Do you want to truly… live?” (**locked**)
- Base completion reward stated in the opening: 1,000 reward points per survived scenario.
- Return to the real world is initially stated to cost 50,000 reward points.
- Return to Origin permanently preserves redeemed items, attribute enhancements, created life-forms, summoned life-forms, and bodily enhancement mutations.
- Six ordinary-human base attributes are initially stated to average 100 each.
